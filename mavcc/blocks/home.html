What the chamber actually does for your business

Six areas of work, each built around a problem veteran business owners told us they were solving alone.

Educational programs and workshops

Sessions on starting a business, securing government contracts, marketing, and leadership development. Corporations can sponsor or teach, and several do.

Networking with owners and corporate leaders

Access to veteran entrepreneurs, business leaders, and corporate sponsors — whether you run a two-person shop or you're looking to partner with veteran-owned firms.

Federal contracts and grants

Guidance on the contracting and grant opportunities reserved for veteran-owned businesses, and help getting through VA certification.

Corporate sponsorship

Sponsorship packages that put your company in front of the veteran business community through events, promotion, and branding.

Visibility at events and conferences

Annual business summits and community events where members and sponsors show their services to an audience that is there to buy.

Veteran-friendly employment resources

A job board that helps members hire veterans, and helps corporate sponsors recruit from a pool of people who already know how to lead.

We're on a mission

To strengthen and enrich the lives of transitioning military, veteran business owners, and their families through economic development, wellness education, and integrative support services — promoting veteran entrepreneurship as a route to self-sufficiency and community sustainability.
