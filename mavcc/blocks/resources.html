How Match Awards works

The platform matches government and business contracts, businesses and employees, and businesses and investors. Media entities and consultants find their matches too.

An AI-assisted matching system weighs key terms, NAICS codes, PSC codes, and ZIP codes, so every connection is relevant. Businesses discover contracts, grants, investors, and partners; governments award contracts more judiciously.

Access Match Awards (members only)

What membership unlocks

  • Matching with government contracts and award winners
  • Matching with grants, grant winners, and sub-award winners
  • Matching with microloans and grants
  • Business-to-business opportunities posted by other entities
  • Partner suggestions for non-GSA Schedule holders
