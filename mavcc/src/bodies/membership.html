If you served in the U.S. military and your company is at least 51% owned by former service members, you can join under Veteran Owned Business status. VA certification can be difficult, and the chamber will walk you through it.

Note: joining as a self-identified Veteran Owned Business carries no weight in federal grant applications or any official status outside the chamber.

The VA's full requirements:

  • Ownership. At least 51% owned by one or more veterans, directly and unconditionally.
  • Control. Veterans run day-to-day operations and long-term decisions, and hold the highest officer position.
  • Veteran status. Active duty service, discharged under conditions other than dishonorable. Proof is usually DD Form 214.
  • Business type. Small under SBA size standards, for-profit, and registered as a U.S. legal entity.

Programs such as the VA's Vets First Verification Program and the SBA's VOSB program require documentation before you can access reserved federal contracts or grants.
