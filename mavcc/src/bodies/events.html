Business networking social at Punch Bowl Social

Every 3rd Thursday 6:00 – 9:00 PM Punch Bowl Social, The Battery

Join veteran business owners, entrepreneurs, professionals, and community leaders for an evening of connections in a relaxed, business-casual setting. Grow your network, exchange ideas, and find new opportunities.

What to expect

  • Networking with veteran professionals and business leaders
  • Meet entrepreneurs, executives, and community partners
  • Games, food, and drinks available for purchase

Who should attend

  • Veteran business owners
  • Active-duty, reserve, and former military professionals
  • Corporate partners and community supporters

Dress is business casual. No RSVP required — just show up and bring a friend.

Fundraising for local veterans

Mighty Hero Homes fundraiser

28 September 2026

No veteran should have to endure homelessness, yet too many service members lack permanent, affordable housing. Mighty Hero Homes exists to change that — building safe, affordable homes nationwide.

We're proud to organise this alongside them. Their housing bases include health services, training, career centres, gardens, and service dog programs, all within gated, supportive communities built for veterans.
